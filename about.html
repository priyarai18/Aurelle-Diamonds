Our Story

Beauty that outlives a moment.

Aurelle Diamonds began with a simple belief: the most meaningful jewelry should feel as personal as the story behind it. Since 2012, our New York house has brought together discerning clients, exceptional diamonds, and the enduring hand of the artisan.

We are inspired by the clarity of a beautiful stone, the precision of a perfect setting, and the intimacy of a piece worn close for decades.
